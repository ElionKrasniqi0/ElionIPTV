Help

Add an M3U playlist

Open Add Playlist. Paste a playlist address (http or https) or the playlist text itself. ELION only shows channels from the playlist you add.

Live TV

Choose a category, then a channel. All Channels lists every channel in the active playlist. Hold Down to move through long lists.

Favorites

Use the star beside a channel to save it. Open Favorites from the home screen to see only those channels.

Video player

OK shows the controls. Previous and Next change channel. Up and Down also change channel. CH+ and CH− do the same when the TV sends those buttons to the app. Type a channel number to jump to it. The channel opens as soon as that number cannot be a longer channel. Press OK to open the number on screen. Backspace removes a digit. Back clears the number, then returns to the list. Playback depends on the stream and this TV.

Refresh playlists

Reload downloads the active playlist again when it was added from a web address. Favorites and settings stay. If the download fails, the channels already saved remain.

Troubleshooting

If a channel will not play, try the next channel or Reload. Check that the TV is online and that the playlist address is still valid. A pasted playlist has no address to refresh — add it again from Add Playlist. Codec and DRM support depend on the television.

No internet connection — saved playlists and channels remain available.
